ABOUT PULSEOS

From system data to device intelligence.

PulseOS combines monitoring, diagnosis, prediction and safe maintenance into one local-first workflow.

MONITOR→UNDERSTAND→PREDICT→HEAL
Existing tools show data. PulseOS turns data into decisions.
ONE PLATFORM, EVERY SIGNAL

Everything already in PulseOS — plus intelligence around it.

◉

Live Device Health

CPU, RAM, temperature, threads, clocks, battery, processes and hardware telemetry.

↯

Smart Router

Understand resource-draining processes and make informed performance decisions.

◈

Storage Healer

Analyze storage, find build artifacts and reclaim safe space with guided cleanup.

⇄

Offline Converter

Document, image, archive, media and ebook conversion without uploading files.

Local AI
